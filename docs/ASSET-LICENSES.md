# Asset provenance

Architecture, furniture, sculpture, relief art, stone/wordmark texture generation, lighting and CSS fallback are original procedural creations in the project source. No external GLB, image, video, HDR or paid asset is needed. The source geometry definitions are the required 3D assets.

Fonts are installed from @fontsource and served locally:

- Cormorant Garamond — SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Manrope — SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Supplied licenses are copied to `docs/licenses/`. Font authors retain their rights; respective dependencies retain their own licenses. Business facts were paraphrased. No source photography or reviews were reproduced. No business permission or endorsement is claimed; obtain owner approval and reconfirm details before future publication.
