## Visual direction

No authoritative brand guidelines or licensed logo artwork were available. The charcoal/champagne/ivory palette, wordmark, typography, architecture and sculpture are a proposed identity, not verified MASSI branding.

Design references are general luxury editorial/architectural principles: serif/sans contrast, restrained composition, warm metal, stone, dark plaster, indirect lighting and camera-led spatial storytelling. No third-party website, salon photograph, model, HDR or logo was copied. No reviews, awards, experience claims or prices are reproduced. The site clearly identifies itself as an independent, unsolicited concept.
